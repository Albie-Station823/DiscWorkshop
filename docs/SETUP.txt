Disc Workshop 1.8.3
Made by Albert Mark Burdge

This installer installs Disc Workshop and its user guide for your Windows account.
Windows 11 is required. Administrator access is not normally needed.

IMPORTANT: Video conversion, disc-image creation and burning need separate tools:
- FFmpeg and FFprobe: conversion and movie analysis.
- FFplay: video and audio playback in the break preview; keep beside FFmpeg.
- VCDImager: creation of burnable BIN/CUE images.
- ImgBurn: writing those images to a CD-R.

These third-party tools are NOT included in this download. Read the installed
User Guide for download sources and specific setup steps before your first job.

The app only processes readable, unencrypted files. It does not unlock protected
DVDs, Blu-rays or UHD discs. Output is standard-definition VCD, SVCD or XVCD,
not DVD, Blu-ray or 4K output. A compatible CD writer and player are required.

Version 1.8.3 adds large-text high-contrast accessibility, per-disc bitrate fitting,
subtitle burn-in, and a source preview for choosing manual disc breaks.
The preview accepts HH:MM:SS and Space plays/pauses. Choose MPEG-1 XVCD or
MPEG-2 SVCD for one-disc or multi-disc fitting, with optional custom parameters.
Arrow-key preview shortcuts are disabled. Optional disc-break
fades affect both picture and sound. Low vision can be toggled during conversion.
It also prefers sustained quiet audio for automatic disc breaks, especially during
black frames. This is volume detection, not speech recognition. Preview each
break before burning; dialogue-free or story-appropriate breaks are not guaranteed.

Uninstall using Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps > Disc Workshop.
Your converted movies and source files are not removed by uninstalling.
